The three-click swing

Click, tap or press Space to start the swing. The needle runs right through green, yellow and red: click again to lock power. Green is easy with a wide sweet spot. Red pushes past 100% for up to 10% extra carry, but the needle comes back faster and the sweet spot is tiny. It swings back left: click a third time as it crosses the white line. Early pulls and hooks the ball left, late pushes and slices it right. The small blue bar is the perfect power for your target. It allows for wind, uphill or downhill, and the break on putts. Lock your power inside it for a perfect hit.

Every hole has a signature

Island greens, lakes, creeks, bunker fields, sand barrens, boulder runs and lone trees in the fairway. Each hole is named on the tee and plays differently.

Reading the conditions

The wind arrow points where the wind is blowing, relative to your aim. On and around the green, a grid of slope lines appears: white is flat, green gentle, yellow moderate, red steep. The drifting dots roll the way the ball will break. The map also shows arrows pointing downhill. Rough, bunkers and snow all cost distance.
